First release of the shared adapter contracts: `ConductorSender`, `ConductorReceiver`, `SendMessage`, `SendResponse`, `ReceivedMessage`, the `ConductorLogger` shape with `noopLogger` and `createConsoleLogger`, `defineSender` and `createSender` for describing and instantiating senders from validated configuration, and the `ConductorError`, `ConfigurationError` and `NotReadyError` classes.
